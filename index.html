Privacy Policy

SatFinder Pro — Satellite Dish Alignment App

Effective date: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SatAlign Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SatFinder Pro mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate satellite alignment guidance, the App may access the following device data:

Data TypePurpose
Device location (GPS)Calculate satellite azimuth, elevation, and alignment angles for your position
Compass / magnetometerDetermine device orientation for real-time dish alignment
Camera (if used for AR alignment view)Overlay alignment guidance on the live camera view
Device informationApp diagnostics, crash reporting, and performance improvements

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g. through a support request).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate and display satellite positions relative to your location
  • To provide real-time alignment guidance (compass/AR view)
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests if you contact us directly

3. Data Storage & Sharing

Location and sensor data used for alignment calculations are processed on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

If the App uses any third-party services (e.g. crash analytics, satellite database APIs), those providers may process limited technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions, each used strictly for its stated purpose:

  • Location — required to calculate satellite alignment for your position
  • Camera — required only for the AR/live alignment overlay feature
  • Internet — required to fetch satellite position data, if applicable

You can disable these permissions at any time in your device settings, though this may limit app functionality.

5. Data Retention

Since alignment data is processed locally and not stored on our servers, we do not retain your location or sensor data after your session ends.

6. Children's Privacy

SatFinder Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

You may request information about data associated with your use of the App, or request deletion of any data we hold, by contacting us using the details below.
